Find and vet references. Write a REFS.md table (Ref, Tier, What it gives, Our checks, Verdict) with 3 vetted rows or
more, each with a tier (R, A, B or C) and a verdict (keep, flag or reject). Add to PLAN.md a "Refs:" line naming the
REFS.md files and a "Short look:" line saying what you measured in the clips. References are studied and measured,
never traced or copied.

Cite each reference by its authors, year and title when it is published research, a dataset or recorded footage. A
craft reference is never named, by title or by author: its row says what it shows in your own words, with the tier B.
Every row is your own words: quote at most one sentence of a source.

Numbers: write (unchecked) right after every number you did not read in the source itself during this shot, such as a
value you remember or one taken from a summary: "knee about 5 deg (unchecked) at contact". A number you read and
measured in the source carries no mark. The author sees the marks on the live page, and a number stays unchecked until
someone reads it in the source.
