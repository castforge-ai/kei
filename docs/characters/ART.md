# How the character art was made

Kei and Keira are evaluation characters developed for Castforge by the processes described below. Rights in generated
elements depend on applicable law and on the terms of the services used to make them. This note does not claim that every
generated element has exclusive copyright protection, and the repository's licence grants only the rights its
contributors hold. This note says how each piece of their art was made.

- **The character sheets** (`docs/characters/kei-castforge-character-v2.png` and
  `docs/characters/keira-castforge-character-v1.png`) were generated with ChatGPT's image model (OpenAI) from the
  author's own text prompts. No image of another character, person or product was given as input. The author chose
  the design and directed each version: a jointed wooden-mannequin build, the proportions, the poses and the hands.
- **The web images** (`docs/characters/web/kei.jpg` and `keira.jpg`) are smaller copies of those sheets.
- **Kei's built-in characters** (`kei/characters/kei.json` and `keira.json`) are data, not images: the proportions and
  the shape of each body part were measured from the sheets against a height of 1.71 m and written by Kei's own code.
- **The walk cycles** (`docs/characters/web/kei-walk.gif` and `keira-walk.gif`) were animated by Kei and rendered by
  Kei. The figures in them are 3D models that the author made from the sheets with Tripo's image-to-3D tool. Those 3D
  models are not in this repository.
- **The chest mark** is the Castforge logo. The MIT licence in LICENSE covers this repository's code and content; it
  gives no right to use the Castforge or Kei Animator names or the Castforge logo as your own marks.
